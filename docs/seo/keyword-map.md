## Rules

1. **One keyword, one page.** Two pages chasing `כורסא מסתובבת` will beat each other, not the competition.
2. **Every blog post links to 2–3 products and its parent collection.** Content that does not route to a product page is a hobby.
3. **Every collection page is a guide first, a grid second.** Nearly every head term in this category has *informational* intent — Google wants to rank a guide.
4. **Publish for March in December.** Every term in the category peaks in March and troughs in February.
5. **Re-measure monthly.** Volumes and positions in this file are a 27 July 2026 snapshot.
